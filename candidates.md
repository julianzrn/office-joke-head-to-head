## Why the selected candidate wins

Everyone who has ever worked in an office has met this printer, and the brief names the deadline jam as a canonical shared experience. The setup states the familiar complaint plainly; the turn reframes the jam as competence — the printer is the only one who understands prioritization — and the punchline word lands last. It is a real joke with a setup and a turn, not a complaint, and the reframing is fresh rather than a line already going round the office.
